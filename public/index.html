Good to know

        
Questions, answered.

      

      

        

          How quickly can someone reach me?+
          
Same-day slots are usually available in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and next-day across the other emirates. Send a booking request before 6 PM and we’ll confirm your visit that evening.

        

        

          Do you bring materials and spare parts?+
          
Yes — our crews carry the most common parts on board. If your job needs something specialist, we’ll quote it transparently before ordering, with no markup surprises.

        

        

          How does the 30-day guarantee work?+
          
Simple: if anything we worked on isn’t right within 30 days of the visit, we return and fix it free of charge. For annual plans, the guarantee extends across the full contract.

        

        

          How are annual care plans priced?+
          
Every property is different, so every plan starts with a free assessment. We walk through your home, map the year’s visits, and give you one fixed annual price — no hourly billing.

        

        

          Is pest control safe for kids and pets?+
          
Yes. We use municipality-approved, low-toxicity products applied with precision, and we’ll tell you exactly which rooms to air afterwards and for how long.
